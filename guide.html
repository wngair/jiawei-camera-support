How to use

  1. Choose Camera to take a photo, or Edit to import one photo with the system picker.
  2. Choose Original, Warm Day, Green Field, Cool Street or Silver Mono.
  3. Adjust color, grain, vignette, soft glow, light leak, scratches and dust. Each can be set to zero. Original bypasses film effects.
  4. Hold the photo to compare the original. Randomize changes the defect arrangement while preserving intensities.
  5. Choose a watermark and save to Photos. JPEG is the default; HEIC is available in Settings.

Capture controls

Switch front and rear cameras, choose supported zoom stops, hold the zoom row for continuous zoom, set flash, toggle the grid, tap to focus and adjust EV. Unavailable hardware controls display a reason. Front preview is mirrored; saved selfies are not mirrored by default. Capture saves original and processed files by default.

Saving and recovery

Wait for the saved confirmation. If saving fails, retry from the same screen or Settings → Pending photos. Keep the app installed until pending files are saved. Imported source files are preserved, and full-size output is generated separately.

If a previous save outcome cannot be confirmed after an interruption, check Photos first. In Pending photos, retry only when the photo is missing, or confirm it is already saved to clear the local pending copy. The list includes a photo preview to help identify it.

Permissions and language

Camera permission is requested on use. Add-only Photos permission is requested on saving. Allow rejected permissions in iOS Settings. The app follows the system app language setting for Chinese and English.

使用说明

  1. 进入拍摄页拍照，或在编辑页通过系统选择器导入单张照片。
  2. 选择原图、暖日、青野、冷街或银影。
  3. 调整色彩、颗粒、暗角、柔光、漏光、划痕与灰尘。各项可调到零，原图模式绕过胶片效果。
  4. 长按照片比较原图，重新随机只改变瑕疵布局，保留强度。
  5. 选择水印并保存到相册。默认 JPEG，设置中可选择 HEIC。

拍摄操作

切换前后摄，选择支持的变焦档位，长按变焦行打开连续变焦，设置闪光与网格，点按对焦并调整 EV。不支持的硬件能力会说明原因。前摄预览镜像，成片默认不镜像。默认同时保存原片和成片。

保存与恢复

等待保存完成提示。失败后在原页面重试，或进入设置中的待保存照片。待保存文件成功落库前请保留应用。导入源文件完整保留，另外生成原尺寸成片。

如果保存中断后结果未能确认，请先检查相册。在待保存列表中，确认照片不存在后再重试；已经保存的照片可移出列表，只清理应用中的待保存副本。列表提供照片预览，方便核对。

权限与语言

使用时申请相机权限，保存时申请相册添加权限。拒绝后可在 iOS 设置中恢复。中文和英文跟随系统应用语言设置。
