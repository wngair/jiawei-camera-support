Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-30 · Covers Jiawei Camera V1.

Jiawei Camera processes photos on your iPhone. The app does not upload your photos, create accounts, collect analytics, display advertising, or include third-party tracking SDKs.

Permissions

Camera access is used when taking photos. Add-only Photos permission is requested when saving. Import uses Apple's system photo picker and does not require access to your entire photo library.

Local storage

App preferences, imported source files, and recipes are stored in the app's local storage. Failed save jobs retain their source and recipe for retry. Successful jobs are removed from the retry queue. The app retains a recent output for its thumbnail. Imported originals are not edited in place. Uninstalling removes app storage; photos already saved to Photos remain.

Photo metadata

Original photos retain their metadata. Processed photos retain available capture metadata, including any location metadata already present in the source. Sharing a photo through other apps may share this metadata. No capture date or settings are invented when fields are missing.

External support

This support website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Opening external support or submitting a public GitHub issue uses GitHub's services and policies. Information you choose to submit in an issue is public. The app does not automatically send photos or diagnostics to GitHub.

Contact and changes

Contact the developer through GitHub support. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised date.

隐私政策

2026-09-30 生效，适用于佳伟相机 V1。

佳伟相机在你的 iPhone 上处理照片。应用不上传照片，不创建账号，不收集使用分析，不展示广告，也不接入第三方追踪 SDK。

权限

拍摄时使用相机权限。保存时申请相册添加权限。导入使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，无需读取整个相册。

本地文件

偏好、导入的源文件和配方保存在应用本地。失败的保存任务保留源文件与配方供重试，成功后从待重试队列移除。应用保留最近成片用于缩略图。导入原件不会被改写。卸载会删除应用本地文件，已保存到相册的照片保留。

照片元数据

原片保留自身元数据。成片保留可用拍摄元数据，包含源照片已有的位置数据。通过其他应用分享照片时，这些元数据可能一同分享。字段缺失时不会编造日期或拍摄参数。

外部支持服务

支持网站使用 GitHub Pages。打开支持网站或提交 GitHub 公开问题时会使用 GitHub 服务，适用其政策。你主动提交到公开问题的信息可以被公众查看。应用不会自动向 GitHub 发送照片或诊断数据。

联系与更新

通过GitHub 支持入口联系开发者。政策更新将在本页注明日期。
